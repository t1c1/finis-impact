Roadmap

Three phases to planetary scale

Each phase unlocks revenue and credibility for the next. Sell orbit safety now, earn the right to defend Earth later.

V1: Now

Orbit Safety

Ingest SSA catalogs (18th SDS), TLEs, operator ephemerides. Compute conjunction assessment (Pc), covariance propagation, screening. Output ranked alerts with recommended maneuvers, delta-v, fuel cost, and miss distance. Deliver via API, dashboard, email/webhooks.

V2: Expand

Autonomous Maneuvers

Integrate with flight software and ops consoles. One-click burn plans with closed-loop verification post-maneuver. Debris avoidance and traffic management layer for constellation operators.

V3: Defend

Planetary Defense

NEO ingestion from NASA CNEOS. Impact probability pipelines, scenario simulation. Deflection playbooks (kinetic impactor, targeting windows, mission design partners). The DART mission proved it works. We build the operational layer.
